Click the screen to give it the keyboard. Keys are sent as Apple //e keys: letters, Space, Return, Esc, Tab and arrows. Gamepad stick or D-pad: joystick; A, B and X: buttons 0, 1 and 2. On a touch screen, the stick is the joystick, A and B are buttons 0 and 1, Return and Esc are keys, and Keyboard opens the device's keyboard for typing.

Runs the game's own 65C02 program on the Appletini development machine model, with Mockingboard/Phasor sound and SSI-263 speech. This is an emulator: its timing is a model, not the board. Player licensed under the GPL-3.0; its source.
